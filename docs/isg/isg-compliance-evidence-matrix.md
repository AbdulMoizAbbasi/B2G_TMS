## OWASP reference baseline

OWASP identifies ASVS 5.0.0 as its stable release and OWASP Top 10:2025 as its 2025 risk list. This pack uses a targeted Level 2 ASVS application-control subset for review planning only; it does not claim certification or full conformance. Refer to [ASVS downloads](https://asvs.owasp.org/downloads/) and [OWASP Top 10:2025](https://top10.owasp.org/2025/).
